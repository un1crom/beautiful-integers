Beautiful Integers: Phase 1 exploration plan

Goal
- Use OEIS + built-in Wolfram number theory/algebra tools to study aesthetic structure in integer sequences.
- Build a reproducible first research phase on 5 sequences, then scale the beauty hunt.

Chosen phase-1 sequences
- A000045 Fibonacci numbers
- A000040 Prime numbers
- A000041 Partition numbers
- A005132 Recaman sequence
- A000002 Kolakoski sequence

Beauty lenses (exploratory)
- Compressibility: how concise the terms are under compression.
- Delta-sign entropy: local unpredictability of direction changes.
- Growth drama: volatility of log-magnitude growth.
- Residue structure: patterning in modular classes.
- Novelty: repetition vs invention of new values.
